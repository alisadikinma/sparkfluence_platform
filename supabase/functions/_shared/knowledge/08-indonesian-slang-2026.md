## Code-Mixing Pattern

**Formula:** `[Bahasa] + [English buzzword] + [Bahasa] + [particle]`

**Examples:**
- "Gue literally capek banget hari ini sih"
- "Basically, dia tuh orangnya chill bet deh"

**English Insertions:** literally, basically, vibes, prefer, anyway, kinda, which is
